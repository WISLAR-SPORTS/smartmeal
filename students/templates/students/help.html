Help & Support

Find answers to common SmartMeal problems.

QR Code not working?

Make sure your screen brightness is high enough and position the QR code inside the scanner frame.

Meal already redeemed?

Each meal token can normally be redeemed only once.

Token expired?

Meal tokens are valid only during their assigned meal service period.

Need assistance?

Contact the cafeteria staff or your university SmartMeal administrator.
